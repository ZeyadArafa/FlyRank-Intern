## 3. Ruthless Curation & Rejection Log (Discernment Notes)

To pass evaluation, AI image options were judged ruthlessly against our **Voice Card** (*direct, technical, plain-spoken, evidence-backed, non-fluffy*) and **Identity Kit**. Below are two explicit rejection decisions where AI visual proposals were killed:

### Rejection 1: 3D Holographic AI Brain Graphic ❌ REJECTED

- **Image Description:** A 3D rendered glowing blue brain with floating neon nodes and futuristic data particles.
- **Why It Was Killed:** It screams "generic stock AI template." A Head of Search Operations evaluating an ML search decay pipeline is repelled by sci-fi stock graphics. It provides zero technical proof, creates visual noise, and directly violates our plain-spoken engineering tone.
- **Real Replacement Choice:** Killed the 3D brain graphic and replaced it with a **Real Scikit-Learn Metric Table Capture** (`figures/hero_precision_lift.png`) showing actual holdout classification reports.

### Rejection 2: Rainbow Multi-Color Bar Chart ❌ REJECTED

- **Image Description:** A multi-colored 3D bar chart with 8 bright pastel colors (purple, green, orange, red) displaying precision gains.
- **Why It Was Killed:** Violates the Week 3 **Identity Kit** 4-color palette specification (`#0EA5E9`, `#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#F8FAFC`). The overwhelming rainbow colors distracted from the actual data and made the portfolio look like a generic marketing landing page.
- **Real Replacement Choice:** Killed the rainbow chart and replaced it with a **High-Contrast 2-Tone Slate Metric Card** rendered strictly in Sky Search Blue (`#0EA5E9`) on Deep Slate (`#0F172A`).

## 4. Connective Tissue Style Consistency Rules

To ensure that AI-generated connective tissue elements (background textures, UI container borders) form a single coherent **set** rather than an arbitrary **pile**, all generation prompts enforce 3 strict style locks:

1. **Color Lock:** Palette strictly locked to `#0F172A` (Deep Slate background), `#1E293B` (Card surface), and `#0EA5E9` (Sky Blue accent).
2. **Lighting Lock:** Flat technical dark mode. Zero realistic shadows, zero lens flares, zero 3D metallic renders.
3. **Geometry Lock:** 2D blueprint grid lines, 8px rounded corners, crisp monospace typography overlays.
